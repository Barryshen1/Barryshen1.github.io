Hi! I'm an incoming CS PhD student at the [University of Florida](https://cise.ufl.edu/), where I will be advised by Prof. [Zhe Jiang](https://www.jiangteam.org/). Before that, I received my master's degree from the [University of Washington](https://www.ece.uw.edu/) and my bachelor's degree from the [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/engineering/). I'm interested in LLM for Science and AI Agents. If you're interested in my research, feel free to reach out!

## Research Interests
